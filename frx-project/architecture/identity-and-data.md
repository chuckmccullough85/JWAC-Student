This companion collects the account and report metadata referenced in E2. Access expectations below are the research coordinator's intended policy; implementation evidence is examined on Day 2.

| Account | Role | Reports permitted by the stated policy |

The research coordinator manages registration and assignment changes through a separate process; file/report association and retrieval are planned separately from the current file-intake function.
